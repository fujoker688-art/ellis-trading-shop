Our Story

Bridging Europe's finest traditions with Hong Kong's vibrant lifestyle.

Founded with Passion

Ellis Trading was born from a simple belief: that the extraordinary wines of Europe and the luxury beauty products of France deserve to be accessible to everyone in Hong Kong.

Our founders spent years traveling through Bordeaux, Burgundy, and Champagne — tasting, learning, and building relationships with vineyards and artisans who share our passion for excellence.

Today, we curate a collection of over 200 premium products, from the distinguished Henri de Malrome wines to the luxurious beauty lines of Estebel, Senbel, and Vigne Royale.
